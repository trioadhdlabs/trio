# Privacy Policy

_Last updated 4 November 2026._

Trio has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. Nothing you type leaves your
device.

## What Trio stores

Your main items, their sub items, and your settings. That's all. They live in Trio's own
container on your device.

## What Trio collects

Nothing. There is no tracking, no advertising identifier, no crash reporting SDK,
and no third-party code that could collect anything. Trio does not use your data
to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

## Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data removes every main item and sub item from this device
immediately. Deleting the app removes everything too.

## Children

Trio is safe for all ages. It collects nothing from anyone, children included.
