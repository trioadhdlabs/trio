# Trio Support

## Contact

Email **trioadhdlabs@gmail.com** and you'll get a reply.

## Common questions

**When do my three reset?**
At midnight by default. Settings → Day starts at lets you move the boundary — set
it to 4:00 AM and anything you do before then still counts as the previous day.

**Where did yesterday's items go?**
The Log tab keeps every past day.

**Can I have more than three main items?**
No. That's the app.

**How do I reorder main items?**
Press and hold one, then drag it. Sub items reorder by their grip handle
inside an open main item.

**How do I delete a main item?**
Tap it, then the bin at the bottom of the card. To delete a sub item, drag it
by its grip onto that same bin.

**Does Trio sync between my devices?**
Not yet. Your items stay on the device you add them on. Sync through your own
private iCloud is planned for a future update, and even then nothing will pass
through any other
server.

**How do I delete everything?**
Settings → Delete all data.
